About Me

I am a web design and development student at Stadio, working towards my Bachelor's Degree in Web Design and Development. I build websites and web applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, and I love using my creative side to make things that look good, and work well.


I have stepped up as a leader through my Class Representative position, I am used to working long hours, and I keep both physical and digital documents well organised. I am always eager to learn new things, I just have to be careful not to fall into a rabbit hole that will suck me in for months.


I was born and raised in Gauteng, attending Laerskool Rooihuiskraal and Hoërskool Zwartkop before finishing school through Impaq Online. Switching to an online school after an accident turned out to be a positive change, as it allowed me to focus on my work without the constant external stimulation.
